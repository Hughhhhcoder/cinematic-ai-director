# Roadmap

The roadmap describes intended direction, not promised delivery dates.

## Near term

- Expand qualified visual calibration evidence without weakening the clean live-action floor.
- Add more complete public fixtures for multi-character dialogue, vehicle interiors, and returned-take audits.
- Version Higgsfield adapter snapshots independently from the core schema.
- Improve machine-readable QA evidence summaries and gallery provenance.

## Later

- Schema evolution only when a real production requirement cannot be represented safely in v1.6.
- Additional offline adapters where the target platform has documented, stable controls.
- Optional non-generative inspection helpers that remain portable and do not conceal failed source pixels.

## Explicit non-goals

- Automatic platform login, upload, or credit consumption.
- Claims of pixel-identical ImageGen reproduction.
- Animation, illustration, anime, CG/PBR, or game-art workflows.
- Post-processing that disguises failed identity, anatomy, texture, or lighting evidence.
