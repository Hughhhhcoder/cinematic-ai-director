# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project free from harassment regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led.
- Critique outputs and systems, never a contributor's identity or body.
- Treat reference rights, consent, privacy, and cultural context as production requirements.
- Disclose uncertainty and do not market failed or unverified outputs as stable.
- Accept maintainers' scoped moderation and safety decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, discriminatory body judgments, publication of non-consensual likenesses, deliberate rights violations, or sustained disruption are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private security advisory when confidentiality is needed, or contact the maintainer through GitHub. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or permanently ban accounts according to impact and recurrence.

This code follows the intent of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
