Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: empty live-action feature-film location authority, native 2.39:1 theatrical widescreen, no baked black bars.

PHOTOGRAPHIC EVENT
A real feature-film location unit records an operational Arctic search-and-rescue aircraft hangar forty minutes before sunrise while a crosswind snow squall moves across the airfield. The camera stands at human eye height in a reachable side service doorway, turned about 45 degrees across the hangar toward the partly open main door. This is a physically built working facility, not concept art or an architectural advertisement.

PHYSICAL WORLD AND ANCHOR
A long-span steel portal-frame hangar with off-white insulated wall panels, exposed dark roof trusses, matte sealed concrete floor, drainage channels, two recessed service bays, a suspended maintenance gantry, coiled power leads on dedicated wall hooks and a clear vehicle lane. The hangar itself is empty of aircraft and people. Through the partly open main door, only the distant tail and landing lights of a rescue aircraft are partially visible on the snow-covered apron. One signal-yellow emergency deicing-manifold cabinet bolted to the near right portal frame is the unique spatial anchor. Its pipes meet the floor and wall plausibly. Door tracks, beams, drains, service access and safety clearances remain coherent and buildable. No readable signs, logos, mannequins or human silhouettes.

ENVIRONMENT, LIGHT AND MATERIAL
Snow travels horizontally outside according to the crosswind and is readable only against two real apron floodlights and the dark runway. A narrow windblown accumulation crosses the door threshold; the protected interior remains dry except for a bounded melt strip beside the drain. Cold predawn sky enters through the main door. Four neutral-white industrial high-bay practicals create separated pools on the floor; no decorative fog, smoke, beam or colored rim light. Insulated panels, structural steel, rubber, concrete, painted cabinet metal, glass and snow retain distinct clean material response.

CAMERA, DEPTH AND FINITE DYNAMIC RANGE
Moderate-wide natural perspective, straight verticals, foreground portal-frame occlusion, anchor and gantry in the middle plane, open door and runway deep behind. Exposure protects interior concrete, the yellow cabinet and the door-edge snow. Apron floodlight cores and tiny aircraft landing lights may clip; the upper roof void, rear service bays and sky beyond the runway fall into genuine black with lost detail. Snow outside retains only a narrow readable band around the light pools. No multi-exposure HDR, local tone mapping, clarity sculpting, lifted gray shadows, artificial grain, bloom or teal-orange wash.

No painterly or patch smearing. No animation, CG, PBR showroom or game-environment rendering. No repeated or copied artifact across steel, concrete, snow and panels. No oversharpened outline, halo, fake lens dirt or watermark.
